    Controller

    Buttons: choose one with {ok}, then press the button to put it on ({back} calls it off). A button can do one thing on a tap and another on a hold. Options and the sticks stay put.

    Play

    Nothing comes for anyone: no cats, naughty boys, rugby or cricket teams, Flag Lads, Bendy or seagull muck. Other players can still shoot you. It's for the whole game, and the host's setting is the one that counts.
